# Authority map

Who decides what, per client and per domain. Read it before recording a decision from a meeting or a thread, and update it whenever a meeting shows who decides or who is still learning a subject.

Levels: owner (decides the domain), specialist (knows it, does not decide it), learner (exploring the subject), outsider (opinion).

## Speech acts

- Decision: "it is settled", an owner and a date assigned, agreement on both sides.
- Proposal: a suggestion, a trade-off, "I would do it this way".
- Brainstorm: conditional, "what if", thinking out loud.
- Question: someone trying to understand.

Only an owner making a decision becomes DECIDED. Everything else is PROPOSED until confirmed. When in doubt, demote; never promote speech to a decision. AI meeting summaries that list "decisions" count as PROPOSED until checked against the transcript.
