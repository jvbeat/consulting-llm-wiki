# Decisions: Northwind Grocers

## D1 One program for the three banners

Decided by Dana Reyes (Head of CRM) on 2026-09-02, in the kickoff.

Members earn and redeem in any banner. The banner stays as an attribute of the transaction, not as a separate program.

## D2 Points never expire

Decided by Dana Reyes on 2026-09-02, in the kickoff.

Points stay valid while the member is active.

## D3 Tier review is quarterly

> [!superseded] 2026-09-16 · replaced by [[#D4]]
> Was: tiers recalculated every quarter.

Decided by Dana Reyes on 2026-09-02, in the kickoff.

## D4 Tier review is monthly

Decided by Dana Reyes on 2026-09-16, by email. Replaces D3, because quarterly review left upgraded customers waiting too long for their benefits.

Tiers are recalculated on the first day of each month.

## D5 Points expire after 12 months

Decided by Dana Reyes on 2026-09-30, after finance flagged the liability. This supersedes D2.

Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
